Jiawei Huang

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor at Católica Lisbon School of Business & Economics, holding a Ph.D. in Business Analytics from the Department of Operations, Business Analytics, and Information Systems, Carl H. Lindner College of Business, University of Cincinnati. My research develops statistical methodology for complex business data — discrete data analysis and LLM-based methods for mixed-type, unstructured survey data, alongside machine learning and deep learning for high-dimensional, multimodal data — with applications in finance and organizational behavior.
