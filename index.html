My research sits at the intersection of statistics, artificial intelligence, and business.

I am Assistant Professor of Operations and Data Science at Católica Lisbon School of Business and Economics. I received my Ph.D. in Business Analytics from the Carl H. Lindner College of Business at the University of Cincinnati, an M.S. in Statistics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, and a B.S. in Statistics from Renmin University of China.

My research centers on two themes:

  1. Novel statistical methodology. I contribute to nominal data analysis, configurational methods, and overparameterized models, with applications in socioeconomics, finance, and information systems.
  2. AI adoption for analytics. I study how AI is adopted for analytics, from large-scale text and organizational data to AI-supported experiments and systems.

I am also actively engaged in industry collaboration. Working with several partners, I have developed methods and systems that were carried through from problem definition to deployment.
