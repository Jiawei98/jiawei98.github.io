Jiawei Huang

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Data Science at Católica Lisbon School of Business & Economics.

My research develops statistical methodology for complex business data. I build tools for discrete data analysis and integrate large language models to analyze mixed-type and unstructured survey data. In parallel, I advance machine learning and deep learning methods for high-dimensional, multimodal data, with applications in finance and organizational behavior.
